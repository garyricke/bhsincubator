Fund the Dennis Piron Scholarship for Entrepreneurship

In recognition of Dennis' 10 years as the BHS INCubator Entrepreneurship teacher, we're working to launch a scholarship through the Batavia Foundation for Educational Excellence to annually award a graduating senior a $1,000 one-time scholarship.

Please join us in raising the initial amount needed to launch this for the class of 2027 — and to carry Dennis's work forward, one student at a time, for years to come.

  • $1,000 one-time scholarship, awarded annually
  • Administered by the Batavia Foundation for Educational Excellence
  • Launches with the graduating class of 2027
  • Honors 10 years of INCubator@BHS leadership
